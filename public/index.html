Frequently asked questions

How does the AI work?

When OPENAI_API_KEY is set, generation calls OpenAI from the server only. Without a key you get a clearly labelled template draft, never a fake AI reply. Always review and edit before sending.

Is my data private?

Every lead, proposal and template is scoped to your account on the server. You cannot access another user's records by changing an ID. See Privacy and Terms from the footer.

What languages are supported?

English, Roman Urdu and Urdu (where the AI model supports it). You can also set your preferred language in Settings.

Do you send emails or WhatsApp messages for me?

Not in this version. Follow-ups are in-app reminders. Email and WhatsApp integrations are designed to be added later, and are never claimed before they are configured.

What happens on the Free plan?

Up to 25 leads and 10 AI generations per month, enforced on the server. Usage is shown on the Billing page.
